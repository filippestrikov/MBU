## 1. [BUG] Customer Service> All subject blocks open the "Levering en tijd" subpage

Steps to reproduce:

1. Open https://staging-bulbbarn.mbu.dev/find-the-solution-to-your-question as a guest (desktop 1280 × 720).
2. In the "Snelle links" subject blocks, click "Volg je bestelling".
3. Repeat for "Retourneren en annuleren" and "Bestelling en betalingen".

Actual Result:

Every subject block links to `/delivery-and-time`; the opened page title is
"Levering en tijd" for every subject.

Expected Result:

Each subject block opens its own Customer Care subpage whose title matches the
selected subject. Source: BBM2-56 "Navigate to the corresponding subpage from each
subject block"; BBM2-55 "Update page title when a different subject is selected".
