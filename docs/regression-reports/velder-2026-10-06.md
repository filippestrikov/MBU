## 1. [BUG] Checkout> No delivery date can be selected – order cannot be completed

Steps to reproduce:

1. Log in on https://tst-bestellen-velder-com.mbu.dev/ as the TST customer and add product 188843 to the cart.
2. Open /checkout/, click "Bestelling afronden".
3. Click "Andere bezorgdatum" and browse October–December 2026.

Actual Result: "Bezorgdatum is verplicht" is shown; in the date picker every day of October, November and
December 2026 is disabled. The order cannot be placed.

Expected Result: selectable delivery dates so the order can be completed (Regression checklist #5).
Check whether TST is missing delivery-schedule data for this customer or whether the date logic is broken.

## 2. [BUG] Cart/Checkout> Item prices are € 0,00 – checkout falls back to "free" payment

Steps to reproduce:

1. As the TST customer, open product 188843 (shows € 13,00* per ST) and add it to the cart.
2. Open /checkout/.
3. In Admin, open the customer → Shopping cart tab.

Actual Result: the checkout shows no amounts ("Totaal 1 producten"), the only payment method is the
zero-total "free" method, Admin shows the cart items at € 0.00, and the customer's order history on TST
shows € 0,00 totals (e.g. 11000008607, 06-03-2026).

Expected Result: item prices and totals equal the product price (non-zero) in cart, checkout and order
(V2-9 / V2-73).

## 3. [BUG] Price unit> Product 188843 priced "per ST" instead of "per collo"

Steps: open product 188843 (GRAT-FOL40+ MOZZARELLA 2K) on desktop (1366 × 786), mobile (390 × 844),
tablet portrait (768 × 1024) and landscape (1024 × 768).

Actual Result: "€ 13,00* per ST" on all four; the cart line says "collo à 5 stuks".

Expected Result: "prijs per collo" on every device (V2-56).

## 4. [BUG] Login screen> "Cookies settings" link missing from the footer

Steps: open https://tst-bestellen-velder-com.mbu.dev/customer/account/login/ (logged out) and look at the footer.

Actual Result: "Privacy statement" and "Cookie statement" are present; "Cookies settings" is missing.

Expected Result: all three links "Cookies settings", "Privacy statement", "Cookie statement" (V2-47).

## 5. [BUG] Reset password> E-mail field jumps when the warning appears

Steps: open /customer/account/forgotpassword/, submit with an empty e-mail.

Actual Result: the validation warning pushes the e-mail field down by 18.5 px.

Expected Result: the warning appears in a stable position without moving other elements (V2-71).

## 6. [BUG] Cart page> Typed quantity is not saved on blur or Tab

Steps: with one product in the cart, open /checkout/cart/, type 4 in the quantity field, then click
outside the field (or press Tab); reload the cart.

Actual Result: the quantity is not saved (Enter does save). On the cart page, "Verwijder" also sent no
request in manual checks, and the minicart once showed different quantities than the cart page.

Expected Result: the typed quantity is saved on blur, Enter and Tab; drawer and cart page stay in sync (V2-66).

## 7. [BUG] Product tiles> Rapid "+" clicks sometimes lose clicks

Steps: on the homepage, click "+" on a product tile 5 times quickly, then open the cart.

Actual Result: in one run the cart held 3 instead of 5 (other runs: 5).

Expected Result: exactly 5 (V2-67 / V2-43).
